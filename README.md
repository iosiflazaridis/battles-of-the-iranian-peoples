# Where the Iranian Peoples Fought

An interactive map, the win-draw-loss record of the Iranian side and the full list of the 2,276 named land, sea and air battles
in which an Iranian people led one side or fought in strength (about a fifth of an army, or a named unit): the Persians, Medes, Parthians,
Daylamites, Kurds, Lurs, Baloch and Hazaras, and the Scythians, Saka, Sarmatians, Alans, Sogdians, Bactrians and Pashtuns, from the Medes
in the Assyrian records (c. 815 BCE) to 31 October 2026. Each battle records the Iranian peoples and why it counts, the result and how it
was decided, the confidence of the entry, every doubt and flag, and its sources.

**Open the page:** https://iosiflazaridis.github.io/battles-of-the-iranian-peoples/

- `index.html`: the page (self-contained; the data are embedded)
- `img/`: the 26 images in full size (3200 × 1800); `thumb/`: their thumbnails
- `dl/`: the data as CSV, JSON and an Excel workbook (with the record tables and the entries removed, with reasons)

Record of the Iranian side: 730 won, 68 drawn, 778 lost in the 1,576 battles with Iranians on one side only and a known
result; 700 more are listed but not scored (625 with Iranians on both sides, 65 air and missile strike
operations, 10 with unknown results).

Sources: cited battle by battle in the data. Map: Natural Earth.
